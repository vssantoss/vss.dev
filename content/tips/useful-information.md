# Useful information

[call2recycle](https://www.call2recycle.org)<br>
Call2Recycle® is the country’s leading consumer battery recycling and stewardship program. Thousands of partners, including retailers and government municipalities, work with Call2Recycle to manage their battery recycling efforts. Through our tailored and personalized approach, we offer partners across the U.S. an easy way to address their battery recycling needs, all while ensuring the reduction of hazardous waste in compliance with state and national regulations.
