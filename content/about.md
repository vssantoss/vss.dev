# About

The [README](/) is the short version. This is the longer one.

I'm Victor, a full-stack developer. I like building things that are
small, fast, and honest about what they do. Most of my work lives on the
web: APIs and the data underneath them, interfaces that respond
instantly, and the unglamorous glue that keeps it all running.

## How I work

- **Ship, then refine.** Working software beats a perfect plan. I get
  something real in front of people early and improve from there.
- **Readable over clever.** Code is read far more than it's written, so
  I optimise for the next person, often future me.
- **Own the whole path.** From the data model to the deploy. I care how
  it behaves in production, not just on my machine.

## What I'm good at

- **Backend:** designing services, APIs, and the data layer underneath them
- **Frontend:** semantic HTML, modern CSS, and JavaScript without the ceremony
- **The glue:** CI, deploys, and observability, the parts nobody brags about

## What I'm looking for

A **backend or full-stack role** on a team that values shipping and
craft in equal measure. Available now and remote-friendly.
